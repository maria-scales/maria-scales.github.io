Benchmark snapshots

Every number the Unit Economics Estimator starts from, with its tag and its source, as of a given date. Generated from the calculator itself.
2026-09-07 · v1.1 · Systems · companion to the Unit Economics Estimator

Benchmarks only – the numbers the tool uses before you enter anything of your own. Your plans and prices are the one thing it cannot know. Notation: [B] benchmark · [E] estimate · [A] assumption.

What is in here
  • Every benchmark the Unit Economics Estimator ships with, dated: 32 inputs across two funnels and the LTV block, 6 verticals, 6 geos, 18 public sources.
  • Each input carries its tag – B benchmark, E estimate, A assumption – and the source it came from. Today that is 11 B, 14 E, 7 A.
  • The tables are generated from the calculator's own constants, not typed: a number that changes in the tool changes here, and a number that is not in the tool cannot appear here.
  • One run is one dated snapshot, kept alongside the previous ones, so the record of where these benchmarks stood accumulates over time.
