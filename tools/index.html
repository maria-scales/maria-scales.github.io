Tools

What is in here
  • Two interactive models, both running in the page itself: the Unit Economics Estimator and the LTV Calculator.
  • The estimator answers one question – does this subscription app's growth math ever close? It computes LTV M13, cost per subscriber, payback and the CPA you would have to hit.
  • 24 benchmark inputs in the app funnel, 22 in the web funnel (32 distinct), each tagged B (benchmarked), E (estimated) or A (assumption), and each replaceable with your own number.
  • 35 self-tests in the estimator and 24 in the LTV calculator run inside the page – append ?test=1 to any tool URL to watch them pass.
  • Both grew out of the stealth subscription case: the model was built to settle a real scale-or-stop decision, not as a demo.
