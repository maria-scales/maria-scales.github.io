How the list was built

I collected the list with AI research sessions (Claude), region by region, in October 2026. Each session searched for marketing events country by country and topic by topic, in English and in the local language, and took every fact from the event's own site or the organiser's announcement. Lists of top conferences from other sites served only to find names.

An event is on the list when its main audience is marketers, growth, advertising, media, e-commerce or martech people, it happens in person, and its next edition starts between 1 November 2026 and 31 December 2027, or it is annual and its 2027 date is not out yet. Events run by one vendor are included when anyone can buy or claim a ticket.

Prices are the standard full-conference pass, the lowest and the highest public tier, converted to US dollars on the day of the check; early-bird deals and VAT make the price you pay differ. Audience size is the latest edition as the organiser reported it, or the organiser's forecast for the next one, marked forecast. A number marked ≈ comes from a third party because the organiser published none.

Limitations

Dates move and events get cancelled. Check the official site before you book. 157 of 402 rows are marked high confidence in the CSV: the official page confirmed the dates and the city. The other rows carry medium or low with the reason in sources.

The organiser has published exact dates for 157 of 402 events. 79 more have dates only in third-party event calendars. The rest show the month the organiser announced (20) or the month of the last edition (146). Ticket prices are known for 121 events, audience size for 160.

Coverage is deeper where the market is bigger. The United States, the UK and Western Europe are covered by topic; for small markets I list the events a marketer would travel to and the largest local ones. If an event is missing or a fact is wrong, write to me and I will fix it in the next update.
