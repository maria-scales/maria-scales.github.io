🇺🇸 United States (primary focus)

No subject-specific prerequisites. All biology and chemistry taught from scratch within the degree. Maths+FM+Physics+Chemistry blocks nothing. Admission based on overall academic strength (GPA/SAT/A-levels). MIT, Stanford, UCSD, CMU, Harvard — all fully accessible.

A-levels accepted as Baccalauréat equivalent. No Biology A-level required for STEM Licences (Maths/CS). Paris-Saclay (ARWU #15) Bioinformatics is a Master's — entered after a Maths or CS Licence. No prior biology needed. Main barrier: French language at UG level. See Section 7.

🇬🇧 United Kingdom — different picture

Subject-specific gates: Biology A-level treated as prior knowledge. Edinburgh/Manchester/Imperial have no UG Bioinformatics at all. UCL and Cambridge both require Biology. UK is the outlier — this system does not apply to US, France, or China.

🇨🇳 Chinese universities: to be checked separately — expected finding similar to US/France.

Recommendation Summary
✅ Choose Maths + FM + Physics + Chemistry if targeting US universities
From a US university perspective, this combination blocks almost nothing. Top US programs (MIT, Stanford, UCSD, CMU) teach all biology and biochemistry from scratch — no prior Biology A-level is expected or required. Maths + FM + Physics + Chemistry is an exceptionally strong profile: it signals quantitative rigour, and Chemistry is directly useful since all bioinformatics programs include General Chemistry and Biochemistry in years 1–2. Bioinformatics, Computational Biology, CS, Engineering, and Physical Sciences are all fully open. This is the recommended choice if Andrey is targeting US (or French/Chinese) top universities.
⚠️ Biology only matters if UK universities are the primary target
In the UK system, Biology A-level is required for UCL Computational Biology, Cambridge Biological NatSci, and most Biochemistry/Genetics degrees. But Edinburgh, Manchester, and Imperial don't even offer UG Bioinformatics — it's postgraduate only there. If Andrey is primarily targeting UK top universities for biology-adjacent programs, Biology is needed. For US, China, or France: Chemistry wins every time.
Section 1 — The Two Combinations
Option A — Physics & Chemistry Track
Mathematics Further Mathematics Physics Chemistry
The hardest all-round STEM combination. All four are facilitating subjects signalling physical science and engineering aptitude. For US universities: opens every STEM program including Bioinformatics and Computational Biology — biology is taught from scratch. For UK: strong for engineering, maths, and physical sciences. Chemistry keeps medicine on the table globally (most med schools require it). Recommended for US-targeting students in any quantitative direction.
Option B — Physics & Biology Track
Mathematics Further Mathematics Physics Biology
Also a strong combination, but more specialised toward life sciences. Opens UK-specific Biology-gated programs (Biochemistry, Genetics, UCL Computational Biology stream, Cambridge Biological NatSci). For US universities, however, Biology offers no meaningful advantage over Chemistry — US programs teach all biology from scratch regardless. Ironically weakens medicine applications globally because most medical schools require Chemistry, not Biology. Best only if Andrey is certain he targets UK life science programs specifically.

Section 3 — Program Restrictions by University System

✅ US Universities — Nothing is blocked

At MIT, Stanford, UCSD, CMU, Harvard and all other top US universities: choosing Chemistry over Biology does not block any STEM program. US admission is based on overall academic strength, not specific subject combinations.

Bioinformatics, Computational Biology, Biochemistry, Neuroscience, Genetics — all are fully accessible. The university teaches everything from scratch in year 1. A student with Maths+FM+Physics+Chemistry is actually ideally prepared because these programs include General Chemistry and Biochemistry in early years.

The UK operates a subject-specific admissions system. Choosing Chemistry without Biology does close certain programs at top UK universities. This section is provided for completeness — if UK is not Andrey's target, it is not relevant.

Section 7 — 🇫🇷 France: Paris-Saclay University (Verified)

🏛️ What is Paris-Saclay?

ARWU #15 globally — significantly higher-ranked than most UK universities. Paris-Saclay is a university confederation that includes CentraleSupélec, ENS Paris-Saclay, AgroParisTech, Institut d'Optique, and several grandes écoles. For STEM it is world-class, particularly in mathematics, physics, and computational sciences.

One of only ~15 universities in the world ranked above all UK Russell Group institutions in the ARWU overall ranking.

✅ A-level equivalence: Chemistry combo is fine

France does not gatekeep university programs by specific high school subjects. British A-levels are accepted as equivalent to the French Baccalauréat. For STEM Licences (Maths, CS, Physics), Maths A-level is the key signal — Chemistry is a positive, Biology is not required at any stage of the UG pathway.

The French system assesses overall academic profile, not subject-specific prerequisites. Maths+FM+Physics+Chemistry is an excellent profile.
