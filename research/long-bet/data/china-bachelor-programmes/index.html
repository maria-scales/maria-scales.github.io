Bachelor Programmes Open to International Students at Chinese Universities (2026)

Every bachelor programme in the international catalogues of the universities, with language of instruction and a STEM flag, plus counts per university.
Nov 2026 · Original research · v2026.11 · data checked up to 2026-11-08
TL;DR
  • 1,029 programme rows from the international bachelor catalogues of 17 of 19 universities (923 at 15 mainland universities, 106 at 2 in Hong Kong); 739 taught in Chinese, 224 in English, 64 bilingual, 1 in French, and 1 not_found row (no international catalogue at the Weihai campus of Harbin Institute of Technology).
  • Counted from the catalogues (programmes_summary.csv), the 15 mainland universities that publish counts per language offer 1,022 international bachelor programmes: 916 taught in Chinese, 105 in English and 1 in French, joint institutes included. These are the counts used in the article; the rows above miss the programmes that are counted but not listed, and the 8 English-taught programmes of the Shenzhen campus of Harbin Institute of Technology are rows but not counts (separate admission).
  • 597 of 1,029 programme rows are STEM (is_stem).
  • 5 of 16 mainland universities have no English-taught bachelor programme of their own (programmes_summary.csv).
  • Gaps: the Chinese-taught count is missing for 1 of 16 mainland universities (Central South University: list behind a captcha); 2 universities have counts but no programme rows (Nanjing University and The Chinese University of Hong Kong); 1 lists only its English-taught programmes as rows (Fudan University).

Method

Data for 13 mainland and 3 Hong Kong universities were collected between 9 and 23 October 2026 from official pages of the universities (admission and international-office pages, fee tables, catalogues), governments, embassies and visa centres, ranking publishers and, for stable facts only, secondary sources; a second verification pass on 23 October 2026 re-read the rows with gaps. An independent re-check of 69 random rows against the source found 64 matching; the 5 differences were corrected or given a lower confidence before release. The three universities that entered the ARWU top 100 in 2026 (Southeast University, Harbin Institute of Technology, Tianjin University) were collected on 8 November 2026 into the same columns; an independent re-check of 20 random rows of that collection found no difference: 8 matching, 6 matching on a secondary or previous-cycle page (marked partial), 6 whose page could not be opened automatically. A second check of 20 other random rows of the three universities, read in a browser on the same day, found 15 matching, 1 matching in part, 1 whose page is no longer online and 3 differences (Southeast University's 2027 admission brochure had replaced the 2025 language and calendar rules; Tianjin University names A-Level results in its scholarship notice); the 3 were corrected before release.

Every row carries its provenance: source_url (the page where the fact is stated; several pages "; "-separated), source_type (official, law, secondary), checked_at (date read), confidence (verified, partial, estimate) and note. status is found or not_found: a not_found row records what was checked and not found, with the URL tried in source_url and the reason in note; its value columns are empty. Empty means unknown; no value is guessed.

Text added by the second verification pass starts with "Updated 2026-10-23:". Ids follow the other Long Bet datasets (university_id slugs).

Programme lists were read from the international bachelor catalogues of the universities (PDF, web pages, spreadsheets) for the 2026 or 2027 intake. language is the language of instruction given by the catalogue. is_stem is classified from the programme name: natural sciences, mathematics, computing, engineering, materials, earth, environmental and life sciences and biomedical engineering are STEM; medicine, pharmacy, architecture, economics, business, humanities and social sciences are not. Counts in programmes_summary.csv exclude joint institutes from en_taught_count. Gaps in the lists: Central South University publishes its Chinese-taught list as a spreadsheet behind a captcha (count missing); Wuhan University's 57 Chinese-taught programmes are a subset of a list behind a captcha; Peking University's 101 entries come from an older list (its 2026 notice gives 137 majors); Nanjing University (51 majors in the 2027 catalogue) and CUHK (programme names not given) have counts but no programme rows; Fudan University has its 5 English-taught programmes as rows, while its 62 Chinese-taught programmes are counted in programmes_summary.csv only. Harbin Institute of Technology: the counts are the Harbin campus catalogue (97 programmes); the 8 English-taught programmes of the Shenzhen campus, which admits separately, are rows with campus: Shenzhen in note and are not in the counts; the Weihai campus has no international bachelor catalogue (one not_found row).

Known limitations

  • The Chinese-taught list of Central South University is missing and that of Wuhan University is a subset (captcha); Nanjing University and CUHK have counts only; Fudan University has rows for its English-taught programmes only.
  • The Shenzhen campus of Harbin Institute of Technology is listed from its 2026 prospectus; its programmes are not in the counts, which follow the Harbin catalogue.
  • Catalogues of different universities refer to different intakes (2026 or 2027).
  • is_stem is classified from the programme name, not from the curriculum.
  • Across all files of this package, 1 of 1048 rows is not_found and 1 is estimate.

How to verify

Open the source_url of a row and compare with checked_at. Catalogues change every intake and are the first values to re-check. not_found rows show which page was tried, so a later check can start there. Every URL is listed once in sources.csv.
