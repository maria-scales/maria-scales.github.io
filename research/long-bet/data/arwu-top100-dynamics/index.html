ARWU Top 100 Dynamics, 2003–2026

Which universities stayed in the top 100 of the Academic Ranking of World Universities and which rotated, across all 24 editions. Three tables of my own counts, as CSV.
Oct 2026 · Original research
TL;DR
  • 162 universities have been in the ARWU top 100 across the 24 editions from 2003 to 2026. 58 of them are in all 24; the other 104 rotate through the remaining places.
  • The United States had 58 universities in 2003 and 37 in 2026. Mainland China had none before 2016 and 16 in 2026.
  • Entries per edition: 3.45 from 2004 to 2014 (11 editions), 4.92 from 2015 to 2026 (12 editions). 6 of the later 59 entries are mergers or renames; without them it is 4.42.
  • Three tables of my own counts, CC BY 4.0. The ranking lists themselves are published by ShanghaiRanking, one per edition.

Method and limitations

  • Source. The list ShanghaiRanking publishes for each edition, read from its public API: editions 2003 to 2025 on 22 Oct 2026, edition 2026 on 24 Oct 2026. Places 1 to 100 of each edition, as published. Every number in the three tables is counted from those lists.
  • Ids. Spelling variants of one university share an id. A merger or a rename gets a new id: Sorbonne University (merger 2018); PSL University (merger 2019); Paris-Saclay University (merger 2020); University of Paris (merger 2019); Université Grenoble Alpes (merger 2020); Université Paris Cité (rename 2022).
  • Left edge. 2003 is the first edition. A first appearance after 2003 may be a return for a university that was in the list before 2003.
  • Only the top 100. A university missing from an edition may be just below place 100.
  • Reuse. The ranking is published by ShanghaiRanking Consultancy. These counts may be reused with attribution (CC BY 4.0).
