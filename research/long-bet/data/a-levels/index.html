A-level requirements by STEM programme

What 31 STEM degrees ask for at A-level: Mathematics required by 30 of them, 9 of the 31 checked against live university pages, as CSV.

TL;DR
  • Mathematics is required by 30 of the 31 programmes in this table. It is not one option among many; it is the entry ticket to almost every STEM degree at this level.
  • After it the field thins fast: Physics required by 12, Chemistry by 4, Biology by 4, Further Mathematics by 2 – but Further Mathematics is recommended by 25, the widest recommendation of any subject.
  • Ten A-level subjects appear at all across the 31 programmes. Everything else a school offers is, for these degrees, neither required nor recommended by anyone.
  • 9 of the 31 rows were checked against live university admissions pages; the other 22 are estimates and are marked as such in the table and in the CSV. This is the weakest part of the dataset and it is not smoothed over.

How it was collected

The programme list is the same 31 canonical names as the programme coverage dataset. For each, entry requirements were taken from a UK university that teaches it – the one named in the last column – because A-levels are a UK qualification and UK pages state required subjects explicitly, which most other systems do not. Nine programmes were read off the live admissions page; the remaining 22 were filled from prior knowledge of the same requirements and are marked estimated.

Limitations

Three, and they matter in this order. 22 of 31 rows are unverified: they are consistent with how these degrees admit students, but no page was opened for them. The sample is UK-only and single-university per programme: a different university would give a different typical offer, and often a different recommended list. The typical offer moves – it is a snapshot of 2025 admissions, not a rule. What the table is good for is the shape of the answer, which subject is load-bearing and which is optional; what it is not good for is planning an application to a named university.
