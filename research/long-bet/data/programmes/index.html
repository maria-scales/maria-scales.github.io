STEM programme coverage, ARWU top 100

Which STEM programmes the world's top 100 universities actually teach: 1,258 university-programme pairs normalised to 31 canonical names, as CSV.

TL;DR
  • 1,258 university × programme pairs from the ARWU top 100: 100 universities, 19 countries, 31 canonical programme names in 13 clusters.
  • Computer Science is the only near-universal programme, at 95 of 100 universities, followed by Physics 94, Chemistry 92, Mathematics 91 and Statistics 81.
  • A standalone Artificial Intelligence degree exists at 12 of the 100 – the shortage is not of AI courses inside a CS degree, it is of AI as a degree of its own.
  • Programme names were normalised through 176 synonym pairs (Applied Maths, Mathematics with Statistics and Computational Mathematics all become Mathematics), and every pair is published below so the normalisation can be disagreed with line by line.

Limitations

Coverage is read from what a university publishes as a degree programme in 2025. A programme taught only as a specialisation inside another degree is not counted – that is the whole point of the 12 for Artificial Intelligence. Rank is the ARWU rank of the university, carried over unchanged, and the top 100 has ties, so 100 universities do not occupy 100 distinct rank numbers.
