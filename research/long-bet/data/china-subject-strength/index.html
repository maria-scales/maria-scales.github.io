Subject Strength of 19 Chinese Universities: GRAS 2026, ARWU Indicators and Highly Cited Researchers 2025

Places of the 19 universities in all 57 GRAS 2026 subjects, top-10 counts by region, ARWU indicator means 2010-2026 and Highly Cited Researcher counts without names.
Nov 2026 · Original research · v2026.11 · data checked up to 2026-11-08
TL;DR
  • 1,083 rows = 19 universities × 57 GRAS 2026 subjects; ranks as published by ShanghaiRanking.
  • Tsinghua University is in the top 10 of 20 of 57 subjects, the most among the 19; next are Shanghai Jiao Tong University and Zhejiang University, 17 each.
  • Across 57 subjects (570 top-10 places, ties aside), mainland China holds 199, the US 219, the UK 48, continental Europe 40; the 16 mainland universities of this dataset hold 110 of China's 199.
  • Highly Cited Researchers 2025: 1,423 entries for mainland China and 2,658 for the US; 873 of China's 1,423 are Cross-Field. Counts only, no researcher names.

Method

Data for 13 mainland and 3 Hong Kong universities were collected between 9 and 23 October 2026 from official pages of the universities (admission and international-office pages, fee tables, catalogues), governments, embassies and visa centres, ranking publishers and, for stable facts only, secondary sources; a second verification pass on 23 October 2026 re-read the rows with gaps. An independent re-check of 69 random rows against the source found 64 matching; the 5 differences were corrected or given a lower confidence before release. The three universities that entered the ARWU top 100 in 2026 (Southeast University, Harbin Institute of Technology, Tianjin University) were collected on 8 November 2026 into the same columns; an independent re-check of 20 random rows of that collection found no difference: 8 matching, 6 matching on a secondary or previous-cycle page (marked partial), 6 whose page could not be opened automatically. A second check of 20 other random rows of the three universities, read in a browser on the same day, found 15 matching, 1 matching in part, 1 whose page is no longer online and 3 differences (Southeast University's 2027 admission brochure had replaced the 2025 language and calendar rules; Tianjin University names A-Level results in its scholarship notice); the 3 were corrected before release.

Every row carries source_url (the page the figure was read from) and checked_at (date read).

Text added by the second verification pass starts with "Updated 2026-10-23:". Ids follow the other Long Bet datasets (university_id slugs).

GRAS 2026 places were read from the ShanghaiRanking public ranking API, one call per subject (source_url gives the subject page). Only places of the 19 universities and derived counts are published here; the full subject lists remain the work of ShanghaiRanking and are not copied. A university missing from a subject list gets not in published list (top N), where N is how far ShanghaiRanking publishes that list. ARWU indicator means (arwu_indicators_by_region.csv) average the six indicator scores of the top-100 universities of each group in editions 2010, 2013, 2016, 2019, 2022, 2025 and 2026, read from the ShanghaiRanking API (https://www.shanghairanking.com/api/pub/v1/arwu/rank?version=<year>). Highly Cited Researchers 2025 counts were read from the Clarivate list with its institution, region and category filters, all name variants of each university together and duplicates removed; one entry is one researcher in one category. hcr_by_university_category.csv covers the 16 universities of the first release (13 mainland, 3 in Hong Kong); Southeast University, Harbin Institute of Technology and Tianjin University, added with the ARWU 2026 base, were not counted. No researcher names are published.

Known limitations

  • GRAS publishes subject lists to different depths (50 to 500 places), so "not in list" means different things per subject; read published_list_length.
  • Rankings count research output; they say little about undergraduate teaching.
  • Highly Cited Researchers are assigned to institutions by Clarivate; researchers with several affiliations are counted under the institution filter that lists them. The three universities added with the ARWU 2026 base have no per-university count yet.
  • Ranking data are the work of ShanghaiRanking Consultancy and Clarivate; only extracts and derived counts are here.

How to verify

Open the source_url of a row and compare with checked_at. Rankings change once a year. Every URL is listed once in sources.csv.
