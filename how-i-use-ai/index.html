How I use AI

I run everything here myself, with AI tools, doing the work of a marketer, analyst, tool developer, editor and designer.
Nov 2026 · About

Who I am

I am a Senior Growth & Performance Marketing Manager with 12 years in subscription apps, e-commerce, EdTech and AI data platforms. I trained as an engineer-mathematician, and coding was part of the course.

The principle

  • In any role I watch for the work that repeats. I write it down as a process and hand the mechanical part to AI tools.
  • A written process keeps the data: every run, the options at each fork and the decision that was made.
  • When enough runs, forks and decisions have accumulated, I do a full review and analysis of the process.
  • The review finds the errors, the inaccuracies, the inefficiencies and the gaps.
  • I also revise each process on a schedule, even when it looks optimal, because the environment changes: every week for a new process, at least twice a year for a mature one.
  • Over time I work faster, make fewer errors and have more time and attention for the creative tasks.
  • I arrive at a new company with this toolkit ready: the processes and the tools. The typical tasks take little time, so my first weeks go fully to learning this company's business.
