Hi, I'm Maria – an applied mathematician, and for twelve years I have been growing businesses: apps, e-commerce, EdTech, AI data platforms.

I scale growth and I make it efficient – on numbers, not gut feeling. Into new channels and markets when it is time to grow; more out of the same budget when it has to go further. Wherever the leak is – tracking, creative, the way the team works – I find it in the data first, and I put AI to work on the rest: analysis, creative testing, forecasting.

When the numbers don't add up, I say so early and show why – before the budget is committed, not after.
