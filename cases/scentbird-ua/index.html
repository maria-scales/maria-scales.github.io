How to Scale User Acquisition via Improving Operations
Scentbird · Senior Marketing Specialist · E-commerce subscription · UA operations
27% CAC reduction · Meta spend ×2 in 5 months

Situation

Scentbird is a subscription-based fragrance service that lets customers discover and try new perfumes and colognes monthly in the United States. Business growth is directly tied to active user base growth, so optimizing customer acquisition costs and focusing on higher-margin products is critical for margins.

At the beginning of 2017, Scentbird was a startup, so demonstrating steady growth with all KPIs met – primarily ROI – was critical. I began as a media buyer responsible for user acquisition on Meta.

Task

Meta was Scentbird's major acquisition channel, bringing the most valuable users from an ROI perspective. My tasks were to:

  • optimize customer acquisition costs;
  • scale user acquisition while meeting the ROI KPI;
  • automate routine processes and operations to reduce the FTE needed to maintain the channel.

Action

The main area to improve was operational process – across acquisition channels and the creative and analytics streams. Working with a Meta media-buying budget exceeding $5M annually, I concluded that the volume let us run many A/B tests quickly and in parallel, that even minor improvements would compound over time, and that automating routine procedures would avoid mistakes and free up FTE for analysis and further A/B-test planning.

A systematic approach would surface the bigger picture and the areas to test. I did the following:

  • Audience grid – ranked the main target audiences by CAC using historical data and fixed the audience sets for six-month periods, ensuring we never missed a segment and that segments didn't overlap.
  • No-code automation (first version) – rules to turn ad sets off and scale ad-set budgets immediately when predefined conditions occurred, so we cut losers and scaled winners at any time instead of waiting for manual audits.
  • Day-to-day creative handling – benchmarks and hypotheses for creatives, rules for creative tests and rotation, and rules for relaunching successful creatives.

I formulated three hypotheses: the audience grid would cut Meta media-buying FTE by at least 10%; no-code automation would cut CAC by at least 15% through timely budget reallocation between ad sets; and transparent creative reporting would cut CAC by at least 25% by simplifying creative selection and turning creatives off right after burnout.

Result

  • Audience grid reduced FTE for Meta channel maintenance by 13%
  • No-code automation reduced CAC by 16% (validated by statistically significant A/B test)
  • Increased the share of successful creatives 4× (up to 25%), enabling campaign scaling at target ROI
  • Combined: 27% CAC reduction, unlocking the ability to double Meta spend within 5 months while maintaining ROI KPIs
  • All results achieved without changes to the business model, monetization, average retention, or other core product metrics
