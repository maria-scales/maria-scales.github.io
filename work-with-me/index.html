Work with me

How I work with a company: five stages from the goal to the weekly review, the six parts of the audit, and how often each part repeats.
Nov 2026 · About

Five stages

This is the order I follow when I join a company. Each stage feeds the next one. What I do and what I do with people run side by side.

  1. Goal and limits

    I turn the goal into numbers I can calculate against: budget, payback period, CAC ceiling, and the date by which it has to be reached.

    Output: a goal with limits and a timeline

    With leadership

    We agree the goal, its limits and the timeline before any work starts. Often this is the founder.

  2. Audit

    I look at how everything works now, in six groups.

    Output: the current state, a list of fixes and hypotheses for experiments

    With the team

    Before I start, I ask the people who run the channels, creatives, product and analytics how they work, which experiments they ran, what worked and what failed. When the audit is done, we go through the findings together, and they correct me where I misread the picture. After that the findings go to leadership.

  3. Goal model

    The model answers three questions.

    How does it work now?

    If we keep working this way, do we reach the goal? In most cases the answer is no.

    Then how would we need to grow?

    Output: a plan with dates and stop rules

    Team buy-in

    Each channel has an owner, and the owner agrees to the number in the plan. A number the owner agreed to holds better than a number handed down.

  4. Implementation

    First the fixes from the audit, then the growth strategy by channel, then the experiments by channel. The fixes come first because growth multiplies whatever is left unfixed.

    Output: work that follows the plan

    With the team

    Every fix, every channel and every experiment has one owner and a date.

  5. Every week

    Fact against plan. The results of the experiments go into the log and into decisions. The plan is updated when the facts call for it.

    Output: a new version of the plan

    ↻ Back to stage 2 with the new facts
    With the team and leadership

    We review fact against plan together. Each owner gives their own read of their numbers first. The week ends with a report to leadership.

The audit: six groups

Stage 1 in detail: six groups, each about one part of how the company works now.

Money

Unit economics and LTV.

Tools: Unit Economics Calculator, LTV Calculator

Measurement

SKAN schema, event structure, tracking playbook, and a check of the ad account against the real money.

Tool: SKAN Schema Builder

Ad accounts

Campaign structure and one report across all accounts.

Creatives

Naming first, statistics second. With consistent naming the statistics are much easier to pull.

Funnel after the click

Onboarding, paywall, prices, retention.

Outside view

Benchmarks and competitors: their ads, prices and paywalls.

Reference: Benchmark snapshots

How often each part repeats

The audit is done once in full. After that its parts repeat, each at its own pace.

Every week
52 times a year
  • Fact against plan
  • Results of the experiments
  • Creative statistics
  • Competitors' ads
  • Report across the ad accounts
  • Ad account against the real money
  • Report to leadership
Every month
12 times a year
  • Unit economics, LTV and the funnel after the click, on new cohorts
  • The model and the plan
  • Budget split across channels
  • Review of the experiment log
  • Summary of the month for leadership
Every quarter
4 times a year
  • Goal, limits and timeline with leadership
  • Benchmarks, competitors' prices and paywalls
  • Campaign structure
On an event
when it happens
  • Changes on the ad platforms
  • A new market, price or release
  • A move by a competitor
  • A change of goal or budget
  • An anomaly in the data

Then I revise the model, the plan and whatever else the event touches.
